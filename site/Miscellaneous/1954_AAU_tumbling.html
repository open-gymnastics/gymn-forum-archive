1954 AAU Championships

Dick Browning's Winning Tumbling Runs

Browning, age 19, was a sophomore at the University of Illinois.  He began tumbling at age 12.  He was the 1951 and 1952 NCAA tumbling champion.

1.  RO, FF, Arabian stepout, front handspring, front somi, RO, FF, double back.

2.  RO, whip, whip, FF 1/2, RO, FF, 2 1/2 twist.

3.  RO, FF, full, FF, whip, whip, FF, double twist, punch front.
