Each participating federation submitted a breveted judge to work at the competition.  Since 20 names were received by Mr. Bangerter, the WTC approved the assignment of 16 judges for the preliminaries with four reserves, one at each event.  These four reserves were then assigned to the Finals by the WTC.  Each of the reserve judges was given a responsibility at their event:

  1. Vault - short technical analysis of the vaults performed.
  2. Bars, Beam, Floor - number of medium and superior difficulties including composition of series on the beam and floor.

A draw for judges' placement was not used since the Women's Technical Assembly in Montreal (1976) voted against this and also since the majority of the WTC were not in favor of this method of placement.
