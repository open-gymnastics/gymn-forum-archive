1949 AAU Championships

Irving Bedard's Winning Tumbling Runs

Bedard, age 21, was a sophomore at the University of Illinois (Chicago), majoring in physical education.

1.  RO, FF, FF, 2 1/2 twist.

2.  Tinsica, front somi, tinsica, front somi, tinsica, barani.

3.  RO, FF, full, FF, FF, double full, punch front.

4.  RO, FF, whip, whip, FF, full, punch front.
