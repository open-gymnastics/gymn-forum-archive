Date:    Sun, 2 Jul 1995 00:51:34 BST
From:    ***@IC.AC.UK
Subject: A chat with Natalia Ilienko

While my friend Kasha and I were at the British Womens National Championships
today (1st July) we bumped into Natalia Ilienko the 1981 World floor
champion. Natalia knows Kasha since they sometimes write to each other. So
the three of us began chatting. Natalia herself is looking very well, she
speaks perfect English and she's been coaching in England at the Heathrow
gymnastics club. We asked her if she had heard from some of her team mates.
She told us a few things:

Maria Filatova is still living in Ireland but she said Maria is looking to
move to USA but at the moment she doesn't have enough money.

Natalia last met Natalia Yurchenko in Switzerland, she was still doing some
sort of exhibition there last she heard.

Nelli Kim once came to England and she stayed with Natalia when she's here.
Nelli was thinking of moving to England.

Olga Bicherova is of course still in France with hubby Valentin Mogilny.

That's about all she knows. We chatted for about ten minutes. Before we left,
Natalia and Kasha were talking of meeting up some time so Kasha can go see
her house near London. Meanwhile I also got a nice autograph from her, and
taken a few pictures of her and Kasha together.

Here's some more news that Kasha told me (not from Ilienko):

Oksana Omelianchik has returned to Belgium doing various things. Oksana's
home back in Belarus (I think... one of the ex-Soviet state anyway) is still
very cramped for space, seven people in her family having to live in a tiny
three bedroom flat.

Sherwin

Subject: Oksana Omelianchik

>Oksana's home back in Belarus (I think... one of the ex-Soviet state anyway)


Actually Omelianchik is from Kiev, Ukraine. She has a daughter, Nastya, that
last I heard (early '94)  was living with Oksana's in-laws in Moscow.

Oksana did live and work in the US for a while and was briefly married to a
gymnastics coach here. Her current husband, Dmitri, is not a gymnast but is
involved in the military (I think he teaches at a military institute). Oksana
has been in Belgium working and coaching off and on for at least 2 years.

FYI, Oksana was the co-World Champion (with Elena Shushunova) in '85. She
pinoneered back to back tumbling passes and was the alternate for the '88
Olympic Team.

Oksana's former personal coach, Tatiana Perskaya, is now a coach at Capital
 Gymnastics in Virigina. She coaches '95 Jr. American Classic Champion Gail
Kauchura among others. Gail recently took part in her first major Int'l meet
at the USA vs UKR in San Jose. Along with Tatiana is the former Soviet
choregrapher that put together Omelianchik's well known routine among many
others (some of Boginskaya's early stuff, Sazonenkova's bird like set, etc.).

-Susan
