Date:    Tue, 20 Jun 1995 10:02:06 -0700
From:    ***@ENG.SUN.COM
Subject: Budget Invite - Men's Press Conference pt 1

In attendance: Igor Korobchinski.  John Roethlesberger shows up toward
end of Igor's questioning.  I'll post John's seperately.

Igor speaks very good English, and answered the questions directly
for the most part.  A few times he relied on an interpreter (aka Beth.)


Q: I'd like to ask Igor to assess his performance today overall.

IK: Good, I can do all my routines, but I have mistakes, I think a few.

Q: And how are you newly motivated for 1995?

IK: Because I like this, I like gymnastics, and I want to be in Atlanta
And I compete before Atlanta because I need more competition.  For me
it's very important.

Gymn:  Do you feel more pressure now that '96 is approaching?  With
these meets do you think about them as they are and not worry so much
about the Olympics coming up?

(interpreter)

IK: Every athlete gets nervous, of course.  And whoever does not make
mistakes and has a good program, that's the one that's going to win.
Right now there are a lot of gymnasts performing at a high level.  So
it's very difficult to say who is going to win.

Q: The Ukraine is ranked third in the world right now.  What will it
take to maintain third place and move up in Sabae?

IK:  I hope in Sabae was good, no *be* good (everyone laughs - tenses
are *hard* when you're speaking a foreign lanquage!) And I hope will
have a medal, team.  I don't know what kind, gold or silver or bronze.
But now Russian team I think is best.  China also.  But Japan has
chance.

Q: You didn't go to European Cup and you were second in last years.
Two of your other teammates got to go instead.  How do you feel about
that?  Is that okay with you?

IK: Because I have so many competitions.  And, what about me?  I don't
like it,  I don't like European. It's not real not so many gymnasts.
It's not the same Europe championships or World championships.

(pause)

IK: Okay?  Okay, I go.  (Laughter)


-George
