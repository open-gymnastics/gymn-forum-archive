Debbie mentions that L'Equipe attributes Vitrichenko's poor placement
due to a "mediocre presentation of clubs".

I think something got lost in the translation here.  As far as I
know, mediocre means dull right? If her clubs routine was so "dull"
than how would they explain her bronze medal in this apparatus?

IMHO Vitrichenko displays the most difficult clubs routine of all the
top ten gymnasts today.  My guess is that she had a drop which can
immediately put one out of the running especially in a very
competitive field.
