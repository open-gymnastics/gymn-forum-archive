I don't know what the story is on tickets, but I can find out when I go
home for break (if not sooner).  The venue is the Coliseo Roberto
Clemente in Hato Rey (a section of San Juan -- generally the business
district, but the Coliseo is not in the heart of it.).  It's a smallish
arena.  I think it seats somewhere in the neighborhood of 10,000, but I
could be wrong (could be somewhat less?).  It's not a new building.  The
gymnastics competition at Pan Am Games in 1979 was held there.  The Copa
Puerto Rico gymnastics meet was held there in 1994, and I assume it was
there this year as well.
