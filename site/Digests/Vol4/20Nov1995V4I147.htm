Hey, GYMN,

A few of you have posted questions about IG subscriptions and such lately.

The address for subscriptions and back issues through December is:

IG
PO Box 2450
Oceanside, CA 92051
(619) 722-0030

After that (move date is not firm, but January is likely), it will be:

IG
PO Box 721020
Norman, OK 73070
(no phone # yet)

If you call, have your current label handy (if possible) - it can help us
to have your ID number and so forth.

Also, be sure to let us know if you have moved recently - often people
forget to notify magazines of their forwarding address, and the P.O. only
forwards second-class mail (i.e., magazines) for 60 days. Then, they send
it back to us with a charge. This is true for all magazines, by the way.

Issues mail around the 20th of each month (November was late, going out
around the 25th/26th of October) from our printer in Michigan. They can
take up to two weeks to reach the coasts.

Finally, Ameera asked about the pricing on back issues. Most are $4.50
each, while a few special issues (Worlds and Olympics) are $6.00. If you
buy 10 or more issues total (any combination of regular and special-priced
issues), the regular-priced issues drop to $3.50 apiece, but any special
issues included in the 10 are still $6.00 each.

I'd be happy to answer any other questions.

I'll go now before I confuse things any more.


Nancy

I could not believe it either after watching the news.  Apparently he went
into cardiac arrest while lifting his wife, Katya Gordeeva, on a rink in Lake
Placid NY.  They were just about to embark on an exhibition tour.  He
collapsed and was pronounced dead at about 12:30 AM.  An autopsy will be done
tomorrow, despite Katya's protests.  I can't believe it.  G&G were absolutely
one of the best pairs ever in figure skating.
