Subject: FISH jump

I have no idea what the text is on the fish jump, but it sounds and
looks a lot like a cheerleading jump called a double nine.  One leg
should be straight out (parallel to the ground or higher), and the
other not really tucked, but the toe is at the knee of the straight
leg and the knee of the bent leg is pointing outward.  So basically
you are forming a 9 with your legs.  Your arms go in the same
position; hence the name double 9.  The jump is very difficult to do
properly and would deserve the C rating.  Kelly
