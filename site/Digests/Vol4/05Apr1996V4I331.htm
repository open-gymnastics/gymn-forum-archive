First of all, I think we all need to stop judging Kim Zmeskal.  A situation
is totally different from the outside than it is when you're involved.
Obviously, Kim didn't feel like she wanted to be on the Olympic team again.
When you're in that situation, and you have an out (the injuries), you use
it.  I've been in her shoes before, and if you don't put all your effort
into it and don't want it bad enough, it won't work.  Of course I was
dissapointed with her not making a comeback...who, of her fans, isn't?  But
I've accepted it and respected her descision.  Who are we to say what she
should/shouldn't do?  And as for the "supposed" e-mail sent to that person
from Kim Zmeskal, I guarantee you it was not her.  I have always gotten the
impression that she was a really sweet and friendly person.  I think we, as
outsiders, think of her as an athlete-not a 20 yr. old human being.  We
can't expect her to do something that we all want her to do.  Some of you
need to calm down on this thing...is it really your whole life?  I hope not.
Maybe she's reading this...If she is, then I hope she's happy with what I
said.  Kim Zmeskal is a human being...and she should be treated like one!

The reaction to the posts made by Garfal amazes me.

Come on people, is there ANYTHING left in the world today that isn't tainted
by scandal, corruption, abuse???  HELLO!!!!

I love gymnastics with all my heart, but it hurts me more than anything that
some gymnasts may be suffering abuse and pressure.  The important thing is
to prevent it happening, rather than abusing people for saying that it does
happen.

Only last year a Romanian coach was jailed for causing the death of a 10
year old girl.

But that must have happened because it didn't happen in America, right???
Sometimes I get the impression that Americans only accept the negative side
of gymnastics happens in Russia, China and other former East European countries.

Sure you guys aren't just reacting to something that couldn't possibly
happen in America???

Out of interest, I'm I the only person on this forum not from America who
has read "Little girls in pretty boxes"?  Because I certainly cannot believe
a whole book was written about lies, especially from reputable gymnasts such
as Phillips, Stack, Stokes, B&K Johnson.  While I realise that this topic
has already been covered, the book does contain some facts, eg Gomez and
Henrich are dead, and Kim Kelly was kicked off the Olympic team in
controversial circumstances.

Remember, the important thing is that if abuses are happening, then they
should be stopped, and prevented.  Because if nothing is done, then this
theme is going to occur over and over again.
