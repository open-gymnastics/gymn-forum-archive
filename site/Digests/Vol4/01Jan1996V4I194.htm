Subject: Women's NCAA Video Tapes

I spoke with Meg Stephenson, U of MN assistant coach and president of the
NCAA Women's Coaches Association the other day.  They are considering
offering NCAA National tapes for sale as a fundraiser.  (The coaches
association pays someone to video tape the meet for them.)  If any of you
have comments about this--what's a reasonable cost, would you be
interested, I'd be happy to get some feedback to Meg.  She said they were
also looking at putting together a video on recruiting and the process for
potential athletes as there is much confusion out there.
