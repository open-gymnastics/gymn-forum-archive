interview with Boginskaya. I'll try to translate it and post it if there's

answers that no one can understand how a gymnast nowadays can try for a 3rd
Olympics when a lot of girls retire at 14 or 15. Hmmm. Has anyone hear anything
about this future film?

she decided to make a comeback. And she mentions two things about American
culture that she simply cannot understand: a girl paying for her own meal when
she's out with a guy, and a husband and wife keeping separate bank accounts! I

Date:    Thu, 6 Jun 1996 22:56:30 -0400
From:    ***@INETNOW.NET
Subject: Information on the olympic rhythmic gymnastics venue

To those of you who are interested in the olympic rhythmic gymnastics venue,
here is some information that I hope is helpful. The venue is the UGA
Coliseum. The seating capacity is about 10,000 seats. I have a diagram of
the seating arrangements at the Coliseum. The events are from August 1-
August 4. There are still some preliminary competition tickets available.
Some of these tickets are in odd lots, however. What I mean by that is, you
may not be able to get 4 seats together, etc.

Yes, Jessica Davis will more than likely be the sole U.S. representative in
the olympic rhythmic gymnastics competition. As for the question, "why are
we having a olympic rhythmic gymnastics trials?", it's just a technicality.
Everybody knows that Dominque Moceanu and Shannon Miller are going to the
olympics. So why would they have to compete in the olympic trials? Same
scenerio, same answer. They have to have a rhythmic olympic trials, or at
least combined the nationals and olympic trials. Every olympic sport in the
U.S. has had some sort of "trials" to determine who was going to the
olympics. The various federations didn't just pick some people to go the
olypmics. They had to have an official competition to judge was going. The
same is truth with rhythmic gymnasts.

As for those people who wanted to know is they have good tickets, e-mail me
with your seating arrangements, and I will let know you if have good seats.
