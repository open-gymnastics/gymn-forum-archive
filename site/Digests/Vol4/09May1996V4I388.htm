Subject: PH routine evaluation

>To all the Men's Judges out there, I'm just curious how the following pommel
>horse routine would fit with the current Code of Points and your best guess
>on a score or start value.  Thanks!
>
>First, a downhill pommel circle, step down to two back loops, a spindle to
>downhill circles, two pommel loops to the center, then a Bailey, then a back
>Moore, a downhill circle, and a flair to a handstand on the end.  Then, drop
>down to the center, do an alternate back scissor, back scissor, alternate
>front scissor, front scissor, pick up to double leg circles, a downhill
>pommel loop to backward longitudinal travel, alternating hands across the
>pommels, and then a flair to a handstand dismount.

If I read this correctly you have the following:
Diff-  3 C's 5 B's -                             2.2
Req-                                               1.2
Exe-                                               5.4
bonus-no D's or E's                          0.0
Total start value                               8.8

However, there is one point where I might be interpreting your description
incorrectly, if the back longitudinal travel is over the whole horse instead
of 2/3 as the text seems to suggest then that would increase the value to a D
and get you full difficulty and start the routine at a 9.1.

Hope that helped,
Dean

>1.  It was said, a few years ago, that Dom Dawes was going to Stanford on full

>to Stanford.  Can anyone verify what's going on with her, and why?

Dominique was originally planning to come to Stanford a year after high
school. She was offered admission and a scholarship, signed a letter of
intent, and elected to defer for a year.  Earlier this year (or late last
year) she signed with an agent, immediately making her ineligible to compete
for any NCAA school. As I have heard it, one of the first things they
(Dominique, her mom, and the agent) did was set up a $125,000 trust fund to
pay for her college education. She is taking courses at University of
Maryland while she trains, and I believe that she is still interested in
attending Stanford after she retires. I've heard it's a pretty good school
even if you don't compete in athletics. :)  She would have to re-apply, but
I believe that she has the academic background to get in a second time around.

>2.  How's Michelle Campi doing?  I so much wish that her career hadn't been cut

If I remember correctly, Michelle is a freshman at the University of
Washington and competed beam only this season.  I've heard that she's doing
very well, both in terms of performance and happiness.  Hopefully the NCAA
fans among us will be able to see that wonderful dance ability next season.

Yes, that is three subjects. I thought I would hit 'em all at one time.

1). I saw the same ad for a free gymnastics calendar. I started to send off
for it, but I changed my mind. I figured I would wait and see what happens.
That deal sounds too good to be true. If it is really that good, I 'll
certainly take a free calendar, especially if it is as great as they (the
person advertising it) claims it is.

2). I saw the CBS coverage of the NCAA women's gymnastics competition. It
was very fast paced. I have seen gym coverage like that before though. Our
own local station has shown coverage of the Georgia Lady Bulldogs
(University of Georgia women's gymnastics team). Their shows usually lasted
about 30 mins. But they show all of the Georgia team's competition and most
of the opposing team's. No fluff and few commercials. It's fast paced, one
comp, right after the other.

But for CBS, they should have showed all an atlhete's performance once they
started showing it, not just half, and then cut to another athlete. They
missed up this time. But it was probably still better than ABC and NBC
coverage of gymnastics. At least they spared us the ton of commercials and
half an hour of "people stories".

3). The IRC chat is on Dalnet. The servers I use are igc.dal.net and
liberator.dal.net. The IGC is the better one, if it isn't busy. You should
find somebody on it at around the scheduled hour. After that, you  may not
find anybody.

Oh, I'm glad to hear that Dominque Moceanu is better. But I am surprised she
would be competing at the U.S. Classic. I thought they would just wait until
the nationals in June. But I guess some practice doesn't hurt.
