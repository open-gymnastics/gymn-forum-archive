Date:    Sun, 1 Oct 1995 21:12:00 -0400
From:    ***@RITVAX.ISC.RIT.EDU
Subject: gym support groups

I am a parent of a 12 year old level 9 gymnast.  I have been the
president of her club's booster club for 2 years and am currently
the newsletter editor.  Our booster club which is comprised of all of the
parents of team members is very active.  A year ago we did all of the work
with the two club owners to rennovate a 20,000 sq ft facility for the new gym.
Yes, we did all fo the plumbing, electical and heating - a great group of
parents.  The parents organization has purchased a new floor, tumble track and
is looking at replacing one beam and two bars so we can hold State Level Meets.

I am looking at networking with any parent organizations or coaches to get
ideas for things others have done to help support their gyms.  We do a lot of
fund raising and spend a lot of time in the gym.  One thing we get back is that
we keep the cost of being a competitive gymnast withtin the range of most kids
who want to be on the team.

Please e-mail me directly.
Thanks
Elaine
