Subject: Beam Leaps and Jumps

The Tuck Jump with one leg extended horizontally forward (take off one or
both legs) is a B element on beam (#2.202 in the FIG Code)  It is often
referred to as a "wolf jump" (at least in the United States).  This jump is
often combined with other dance or acrobatic elements for special connection
bonus credit.

An acro-dance combination of B+D receives .2 in bonus credit.  This is why
you might see a front salto on beam (D element)  to an immediate tuck jump
with one leg at horizontal  (B dance element).

Myra

There is no longer a deduction on Uneven Bars for too many elements on one
bar.  A gymnast must, however have at least three bar changes in
international competition.

     The Florida Workout News
     Reports from Florida's Trampoline and Tumbling Happening
     Volume 1 Number 1 June, 1995 Naples, Florida USA

     Florida Workout
     "A Unique Concept"
     "It's not a competition; it's not a clinic; it's a happening!" seems
     to be the recurring theme again this year as dozens of inquiries about
     the event come in from Gymnastics Schools around the world.

     For old-timers, the ambiance resembles the Sarasota Clinic or Muscle
     Beach of the '60s.  One Canadian coach described it "like Toronto's
     "Reach for the Sky" festival at Harbourfront...except the palm trees
     and surf."

     Part of the event is at the facilities of Progressive Gymnastics, a
     state-of-the-art 20,000 square foot gym with a swimming pool, obstacle
     course and activity field featuring rock climbing and zip lines.
     Naples Beach Hotel is the other site with equipment set up on a large
     palm-studded lawn by the beach on the Gulf of Mexico.

     World-class athletes and coaches from many acro-related sports mix and
     mingle with absolute beginners in a relaxed atmosphere of camaraderie.

     While no national Federations are officially represented there were
     competitors and athletes from fourteen different countries present in
     the '94 FWO.

     Throughout Florida Workout it would be very common for a complete
     beginner to work with the coach of a World Champion or perhaps the
     champs themselves.  Evenings are loaded with social events for coaches
     as well as kids.  "Legends" night provides the opportunity to meet the
     greatest names in the sport.

     Whatever Florida Workout is, it's special.  No politics, no pressure,
     just a memorable, fun and learning time for everyone.

     Luxon, Kurys headline All-Star staff
     The 1994 Florida Workout again featured some of the greatest coaches
     in the history of Trampoline and Tumbling.

     Russia's Vladimir Kurys, co-inventor of the ski floor, coach of
     numerous World Tumbling Champions, joined Americans Steve Elliott and
     Dave Green in presentations to fascinated coaches and athletes from
     dozens of tumbling and gymnastics clubs.

     Legendary trampolinist Paul Luxon, often described as the "Greatest of
     all time" presented "Straight-Arm Twisting" and was joined by other
     world-class coaches on the practice floor.  These included Russia's
     Vladimir Pilipchenko, Canada's Dave Ross and Bo Turby from Denmark.

     Aerial Ski Champion Frank Bare was also there to present his BABS
     Bunji spotting rig.

     FWO-'95...bigger and better than ever

     Hosts Craig and Carol Smith and Director Bil Copp have officially
     announced that "Florida Workout III" will be held on December 27-30,
     1995 in the Sunshine State.

     After two highly successful years, Naples, Florida will again be the
     site of this unique Trampoline and Tumbling "Happening."

     Taking into consideration suggestions from previous participants, the
     '95 event will feature shorter workout sessions and more of the
     activities outside at Naples Beach.

     As in 1994, social events will include an ice-breaking party,
     wing-ding and new video premiers.

     "Lost Trick Syndrome" and other mysteries...

     As a part of the service to coaches of all levels, Florida Workout has
     hosted seminars on relevant material by top coaches and performers.
     The '94 Florida Workout was especially rich in talent and information.

     Among the '94 seminars was the world's first organized discussion of
