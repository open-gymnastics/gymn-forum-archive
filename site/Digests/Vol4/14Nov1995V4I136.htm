had been pouting and the coaches had been ignoring her during the

happier when she found the work was worth it (making the world team).  If
it is true that she really has decided to leave for good, it will be a
great loss to the sport-she had a unique style and fun personality that
were fun to watch.  Does anyone know how the Forsters and the Aeriels
team have reacted?  Was it an expected or sudden departure?

Date:    Tue, 14 Nov 1995 21:37:05 -0500
From:    ***@AOL.COM
Subject: Re: Doni Thompson - out?

On November 14, 1995, Cindy wrote:

>Yesterday, I was told she had made the decision to leave gymnastics
>for good.  She wasn't having fun with it anymore.

This *really* surprised me (as I'm sure it surprised a lot of people).  Could
it be that her back injury was more serious than it appeared? I guess
appearances can be deceiving, but, to me, she seemed to be the happiest
member of the U.S. World Team!  All I can say is that it must be something
pretty serious, considering that she is supposedly giving it up a mere 8
months from the Olympic Games, immediately after establishing herself as a
strong member of the U.S. team in Sabae.

I heard on T.V. several times that Doni always prayed every night to "help
make her Olympic dreams come true".  In addition, it is especially puzzling
since she just won her first national title a few months ago in New Orleans -
she really seemed to be coming into her own!

Of course, there is probably much more that went into this decision than any
of us is aware of.  I wish her all the best -- I will definitely miss her
elegant line and clean presentation, which helped to make her a tremendous
asset to the United States team!

Amy
