Date:    Tue, 5 Mar 1996 23:25:07 -0500
From:    ***@AOL.COM
Subject: Collegiate Gymnastics

To All:
     You know, I've been reading back and forth about the lackadaisacal
attitude IG takes toward U.S. collegiate gymnastics, and a thought occured to
me.
     If you look at the top 20 women's teams and figure they have 5 home
meets each, and then you look at the average attendance of each home meet --
then your total number of spectators equals:
     ----------   485,000!!!!! -----------
      That's more people than the attendance of 45 American Cups. That's more
people than USA Gymnastics generates in an entire year in major elite
competitions. And I'm not even including attendance at the 30 other women's
programs or at the men's meets. Add all those up, and the number of total
spectators goes over 600,000 or even higher.
      And, yet, IG and the other major magazines sit back and say, "Naaaaa,
we don't want to cover collegiate gymnastics."
      Geez, if they really wanted to help increase their circulation, they'd
start giving real coverage to collegiate gymnastics and then have "sponsors"
set up booths at collegiate meets to sell subscriptions (from which they make
a commission).
      But, no, I guess more than a half-million spectators isn't worth it.
-- Ronald
