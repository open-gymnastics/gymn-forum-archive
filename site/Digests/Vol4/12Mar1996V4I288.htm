I don't know about Kellee, but Scott "competed" at the Stanford meet this
weekend.  I was concentrating on photographing (mostly Stanford), so I can't
tell you much about how he did.  The "open" competitors weren't included in
the scores, especially since some were doing compulsories and some were
doing optionals.  I can tell you that looking at Scott and Chainy Umphrey
competing on the same team solidified in my mind the fact that there is no
"ideal" body type for gymnastics.  Strength-to-weight ratios may be similar
in these two, but Chainy looks like he could crack walnuts with his biceps,
while Scott....well, doesn't. :)  Seriously, about all I can tell you is
that Scott (along with UCLA alums Umphrey and Chris Waller and Stanford
alums Jair Lynch, Mark Booth, Josh Stein, and Kyle Asano--I hope I'm not
forgetting anyone) did perform at this meet.  Throw in defending NCAA champ
Stanford, California (who have now beaten Stanford three times this season),
no. 5 Illinois, no. 10 Oklahoma, and a strong UCLA club team, and this was
quite a meet!  Results will be in tomorrow's collegiate update.
