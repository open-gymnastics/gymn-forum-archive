Date:    Thu, 18 Apr 1996 23:54:01 -0400
From:    ***@COQUI.NET
Subject: Worlds: Canada Qualifies One Gymnast to Finals!

In semi-final action at the 1996 World Gymnastics Championships, Richard
Ikeda from British Columbia grabbed the eighth spot on men's horizontal
bar tonight with a solid performance to qualify for the final round.
With a score of 9.550, Richard will compete with Vitaly Scherbo and 6
others on Saturday evening.

There were a number of falls from the high bar beginning with a
disastrous performance by former world champion Ivan Ivankov of Belarus
who finished last.  Alan Nolet of Burlington, Ontario overshot his
one-arm Tkatchev by about 3 feet and ended up in 14th place.  Falls by
all other big guns eliminated Germany's Andreas Wecker and Russia's
Alexei Voropaev.

Richard also competed on parallel bars and came a respectable 12th
with a clean routine (9.512) in a high quality field.  He was tied with
Jesus Carballo of Spain.

Richard is competing here in San Juan without the coaching of his father
Mits Ikeda.

More on Richard tomorrow!

Regards,

Grace
