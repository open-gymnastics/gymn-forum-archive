Date:    Wed, 25 Oct 1995 17:13:55 -0600
From:    ***@HARRIER.SASKNET.SK.CA
Subject: URGENT - job opening

Please make this available to anyone interested at your club /
organization etc. and note the deadline date of November 14th 1995.

------------------------------------------------------------------
EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR

The Saskatchewan Gymnastics Association is accepting applications
for the position of Executive Director.  The successful candidate
will be required to manage the office and staff, oversee all
budgeting, be knowledgable of sport profile requirements, and
assist and provide support to the programs being administrated
by the sport governing body for gymnastics in Saskatchewan.

Qualifications:
1. Degree in Sport Administration or Physical Education.
2. Experience with volunteer boards.
3. Gymnastics background preferred.
4. Good organizational and leadership skills.
5. Proficient computer skills.
6. Experience in preparing funding submissions and reports.

This position is a full time position and will be based in
Regina, Saskatchewan.

Salary will be negotiable based on qualifications.

***Deadline Date of applications:  November 14th 1995

Please forward your resumes to:

      Shirley Grebinsky, President
      88 Marshall Crescent
      Regina, Saskatchewan
      S4P 4Y9    (Canada)

We thank all applicants for their interest, but only those
candidates selected for an interview will be contacted.

the following question.  Which commentating pair do hate the most/like the

the Canadian commentation is less biased.  Let me know what you think.
