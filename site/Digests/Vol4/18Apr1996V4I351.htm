Subject: NYT article--Scherbo

On April 17, the New York Times online had a good article
on Vitaly Scherbo, "Scherbo Takes Heart, Seeks Gold
After Wife's Crash."  You can read it online if you catch
it today in the Sports section of http://www.nytimes.com (you
have to "register" but it's free).  Good article, I thought--
it includes some background into the Scherbo's final decision to
move from Minsk to the United States (how they were
burglarized there, etc.), and is very upfront about the
toll his wife's accident took on him.  He gives lots of
credit to Aleksandr Kolivanov and Ed Isabelle for pulling
him through the tough times right after the accident.  Also
very forthright about how the time off has affected his
performance at the PR Worlds.
