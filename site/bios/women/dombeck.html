Carola Dombeck (GDR)

1976 Olympic V Silver Medalist

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  1 Silver, 1 Bronze


Carola was born on June 25, 1960 in Merseburg. She was coached by Lutz Wiedemann at SC Chemie Halle.
