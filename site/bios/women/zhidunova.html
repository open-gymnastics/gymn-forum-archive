Valeriya Zhidunova (USSR)

1977 USSR Youth Champion
1978 Riga International Champion

Valeriya lived in Mogilev and was trained by Oleg Mishchenko at Krasnoye Znamya club. She married fencer Alkhas Lakerbai, and their daughter Karina was born in December 1988. The family emigrated to Brazil in the mid 1990s. Today, Valeria is a coach atInstituto Yashi in Sao Paulo, Brazil, while Karina (a fencer) represented Brazil at the 2008 Olympics and hopes to compete in London.
