Maria Olaru (ROM)

1999 World AA Champion
2000 Olympic AA Silver Medalist

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  1 Gold, 1 Silver
World Championships:  2 Gold, 1 Bronze
European Championships:  1 Gold, 1 Silver

Maria was born on June 4, 1982, in Birlad.  Her father died when she was four years old, and her mother was forced to put Maria into a children's home because she could not afford to take care of her.  At the home, Maria showed a great interest in gymnastics and managed to pass the entrance exams for the famous Deva school.  A solid junior gymnast, she made a smooth transition to the senior ranks.  In 1999, Maria won the world AA title -- only the second Romanian woman to do so.
