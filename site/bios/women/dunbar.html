Diane Dunbar (USA)

1975 Pan American Games UB Bronze Medalist

Diane was born on January 2, 1959.  At age 5, she started taking ballet and tumbling classes.  She took up gymnastics at age 8 1/2, at Diablo Gymnastics Club, where she was coached by Jim Gault and Hal Shaw.  She trained 40 hours a week and called floor exercise her favorite event.  Today Diane Bijesse lives in Pleasanton, CA, with her husband and two daughters.  She works in computer software technical support.
