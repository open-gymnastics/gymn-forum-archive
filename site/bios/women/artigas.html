Marta Artigas (ESP)

1984 Olympian

Marta was born on July 11, 1968. Her coach was Montserrat Ubia. Marta admired Nadia Comaneci.

1982

Jr. European Championships: 24th AA

1983
Blume Memorial: 9th AA
World Championships: 13th Team

1984
Blume Memorial: 3rd AA
Olympic Games: 9th Team, 26th AA
