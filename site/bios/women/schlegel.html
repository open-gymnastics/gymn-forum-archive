Elfi Schlegel (CAN)

1980 World Cup V Bronze Medalist

Schlegel (here with Dick Enberg and Tim Daggett) is now a commentator for NBC Sports.

Medal Total

World Cup:  1 Bronze

Elfi was born on May 17, 1964 and trained at the Xoces-Eagle Club in Toronto with coaches Mary Lee and Geoff Palmer.  She had a successful career as an NCAA gymnast, competing for the University of Florida, and was inducted into their Hall of Fame in 1997.  Today she works as a commentator for NBC.
