Muriel Grossfeld (USA)

3-time US Olympian
1957 and 1963 AAU Overall Gold Medalist

Muriel was born in 1941 in Indianapolis.  After having studied dance for 7 years, she took up gymnastics in 1955 at age 14.  Coached by Walter Lienert, she qualified for and competed at the Olympic Games in Melbourne a year later.  In 1960, Muriel married fellow gymnast Abie Grossfeld.  

A three-time Olympian, Muriel became a TV commentator and coach after retiring.  Her most famous pupil, Marcia Frederick, was the first US female gymnast to win a world title.  Muriel is a long-time member of the US gymnastics coaching staff.
