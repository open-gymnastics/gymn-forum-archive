Camelia Voinea (ROM)

1986 World Cup FX Silver Medalist
1987 European FX Silver Medalist

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  1 Silver
World Championships:  1 Gold, 1 Silver
European Championships:  1 Silver
World Cup:  1 Silver

Camelia was born on March 1, 1970 in Constanta.  She trained at Cetate Deva under coaches Adrian Goreac, Adrian Stan and Maria Cosma.  She was best known for her powerful tumbling and her innovative 1986-87 FX that featured breakdancing elements.
