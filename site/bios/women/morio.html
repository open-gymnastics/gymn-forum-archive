Maiko Morio (JPN)

1985 University Games FX Bronze Medalist

Maiko was born on February 18, 1967 in Kawasaki City, Kanagawa.  She trained at the Asahi Seimei Club with coaches Chieko and Mitsuo Tsukahara.  She has one older brother and one younger sister.
