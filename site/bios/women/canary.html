Christa Canary (USA)

1978 Antibes International AA Champion

Christa was born on June 5, 1962.  A powerful vaulter, she was one of the first women to perform a Cuervo.  Today, Christa Canary Deford is a full-time mom to her 3 kids: son Sean, and daughters Kelsy and Katy.  The family live in Herndon, VA.
