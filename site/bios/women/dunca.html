Rodica Dunca (ROM)

1980 Champions All AA Gold Medalist
1981 European B Bronze Medalist


Medal Total

Olympic Games:  1 Silver
World Championships:  1 Gold
European Championships:  1 Bronze

Rodica was born on May 16, 1965 in Baia Mare. She began gymnastics at 4 1/2 years old and was coached by Elena Marinescu at CSS Baia Mare before joining the national team training center in Deva. After retiring, Rodica coached for a few years at CSS Baia Mare and performed a trapeze act in a circus. She married Zoltan Papp, a choreographer for the Baia Mare Theater, and works as a dancer at that theater.
