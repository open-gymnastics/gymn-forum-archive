Giulia Volpi (ITA)

4-Time Italian AA Champion

Giulia was born on January 30, 1970 in Brescia.  She trained at G.A.L. Lissone with coach Walter Consonni.  Father Carlo is a financial consultant, mother Franca is a literary agent, and brother Riccardo is a lawyer.
