Monique Allen (AUS)

1990 Commonwealth Games AA Silver Medalist

Allen was born on November 10, 1971. A veteran of two world championships and one Olympics, Allen's most successful competition was the 1990 Commonwealth Games, where she won the uneven bars title and finished second all-around. Allen retired after the Barcelona Olympics and worked as a graphic designer. Monique currently works at Gymbody in Canberra, where she teaches adult fitness and flexibility classes. She and husband Wayne Fusca welcomed daughter Ruby on March 21, 2005.
