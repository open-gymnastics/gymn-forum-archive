Elodie Lussac (FRA)

1993 Jr. European AA Champion

Elodie was born on May 7, 1979, in St. Catherine les Arras.  She was coached by Shi Mao at the Marseilles training center.

Jr. Gymnix International:  1st AA
 
1992

Grenoble International:  1st AA, 1st UB, 2nd V

1993
Blume Memorial:  1st AA
French International:  1st UB, 1st B
Jr. European Championships:  1st AA, 1st UB, 1st B, 1st FX, 5th V
 
1994

European Championships:  6th B, 8th V
