Kathy Howard (USA)

1975 Antibes International AA Champion
1976 American Cup AA Silver Medalist

Kathy was born on June 2, 1958 and began gymnastics at age 12 (!).  She has three sisters (Ellen, Barbara and Janet).  Her coaches were Mary Welin, Jessie Arnold and Nancy Bagby of the Hi-Lo Twisters in Oklahoma City.
