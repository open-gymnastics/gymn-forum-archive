Risa Sugawara (JPN)

5-Time Japanese AA Champion

Risa was born on August 15, 1977 in Toda.  She was coached by her father, Hiroshi, who was a member of the Japanese team at the 1978 Asian Games.  Risa trained at the Toda City Sports Center.  Her mother, Takako Hasegawa, competed at the 1972 Olympics, and finished 34th AA.
