Choi Gyong Hui (PRK)

1988 Druzhba UB Gold Medalist
1991 University Games Team Gold Medalist

Choi Gyong Hui (2nd from right) and teammates at the 1991 University Games.

Choi, who's a gymnastics coach at the Pyongyang City Sports Group, was named one of the DPRK's ten best coaches of 2007.

Competition Results

1986
Intl. Championships of Romania:  4th AA, 3rd UB, 3rd FX

1987
Moscow News:  7th AA, 3rd UB

1988
Jr. Friendship Tournament (Druzhba):  3rd Team, 3rd AA, 1st UB, 6th V, 8th FX

1989
World Championships:  7th Team, 10th AA, 8th FX

1991
University Games:  1st Team, 4th AA
World Championships:  9th Team, 23rd AA

1992
Olympic Games:  11th Team, 27th AA
World Championships:  12th UB (semis), 61st B (quali)
