Heidi Anderson (USA)

1979 USA FX and B Champion

Heidi was born on May 7, 1962. She began gymnastics in 1973 at a club in Pittsburgh, PA, and joined the Parkettes club in Allentown, PA, in 1977.  She attended Penn State for two years, then transferred to Oregon State, graduating in 1985 with a degree in business administration.  Married to University of Florida assistant gymnastics coach Milan Stanovich, the couple have two sons.
