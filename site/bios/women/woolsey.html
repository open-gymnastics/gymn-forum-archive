Sandy Woolsey (USA)

1990 Moscow News B Silver Medalist
1991 USA AA Silver Medalist
1994 NCAA Championships UB Champion

Sandy was born on August 15, 1972, and trained at the Desert Devils club with coach Stormy Eaton.
