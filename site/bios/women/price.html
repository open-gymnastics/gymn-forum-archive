Hayley Price (GBR)

1983 British AA Champion
1982  Coca-Cola Intl. V Champion

Hayley was born on April 13, 1966 and began gymnastics at age 7.  She was coached by Hazel Palmer at the Wolverhampton Gymnastics Club until 1981.  In 1981 Hayley finished first at the Daily Mirror scholarship competition and won a month's training in the Soviet Union.  When she returned from the USSR, Hayley joined Redditch and Bromsgrove Gymnastics Club in Birmingham to train with John Reeves (a British team coach).
