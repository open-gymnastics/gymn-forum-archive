Elena Piskun (BLR)

1993 World V Champion, 1996 World UB Champion

"What I like best is the competition itself."
Medal Total
 
World Championships:  2 Gold
European Championships:  1 Silver, 1 Bronze

Elena was born on February 1, 1978, in Bobruisk.  She began gymnastics in 1984 and was coached by Valery Kolodinsky at the Spartak club.  She's currently coaching in Georgia.
