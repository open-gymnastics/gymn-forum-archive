Lucy Wener (USA)

3-Time NCAA UB Champion (86,87,89)

 

Lucy trained at SCATS in Huntington Beach, CA.  Now a wife and mother, Lucy's still involved in gymnastics, as a judge and choreographer at Gaby's Gymnastics in Nashville, TN.
