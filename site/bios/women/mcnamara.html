Julianne McNamara (USA)

1981 World UB Bronze Medalist, 1984 Olympic UB Gold Medalist

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  1 Gold, 2 Silver
World Championships:  1 Bronze
World Cup:  1 Bronze

Julianne was born on November 10, 1965 in Queens, New York.  A good gymnast on all events, she was best known for her work on UB.  During her career, she was coached by Dick and Linda Mulvihill at the National Academy of Artistic Gymnastics in Oregon, by Don Peters at SCATS in California, and by Bela Karolyi at his club in Texas.  After the 1984 Olympics, Julianne attended UCLA and had a recurring role as a perky cheerleader on the sitcom "Charles in Charge."  Julianne married her college sweetheart Todd Zeile, a professional baseball player who retired in 2004. They divorced in early 2015. The couple have four children: three sons (Garrett, Ian, and Aaron) and one daughter (Hannah, who starred on the NBC TV show "This Is Us").
