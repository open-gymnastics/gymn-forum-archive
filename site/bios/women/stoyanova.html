Boriana Stoyanova (BUL)

1983 World V Champion

Medal Total

World Championships:  1 Gold, 1 Bronze
European Championships:  2 Bronze
Boriana was born on July 3, 1968, in Sofia.  She trained at CSKA Sofia under coach Margarit Krasteva.  She is the only  Bulgarian woman gymnast to win a world title (vault, 1983).  She retired after the 1988 Olympics and began coaching.  She got married in 1990 and had a son the following year.  Stoyanova is divorced and still lives in Sofia.
