Leslie Pyfer (USA)

1979 USA AA Champion
1978 and 1979 World Championships Team Member

Leslie was born on January 13, 1963.  She trained at the National Academy of Artistic Gymnastics in Eugene, Oregon, under coached Dick and Linda (Metheny) Mulvihill.
