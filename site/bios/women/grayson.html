Lisa Grayson (GBR)

1987 Kraft Intl. AA Silver Medalist

Lisa was born on October 27, 1972.  She trained at Redcar Gymnastic Club with Jack Grey.  She was the alternate gymnast on Britain's 1988 Olympic team.  She currently coaches at Action Academy in Deerfield Beach, FL.

Competition Results

Blume Memorial:  13th AA
Kraft International:  2nd AA
World Championships:  17th Team

1988

Champions All:  8th AA

1989
