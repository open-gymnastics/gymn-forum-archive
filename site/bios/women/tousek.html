Yvonne Tousek (CAN)

2001 and 2002 NCAA UB Champion 

Yvonne was born on February 23, 1980, in Ontario.  She was coached by Elvira Saadi at the Cambridge Kips club.  She graduated from UCLA and was an important member of that university's gymnastics team.
