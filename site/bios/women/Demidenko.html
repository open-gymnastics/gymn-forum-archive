Taisiya Demidenko (URS)

1937 World Workers Olympiad Team Gold Medalist
1940 USSR All-Around Champion

Taisiya was born in 1917 in Ukraine. A graduate of the Kiev Institute of Physical Culture, she became an instructor there and continued to compete. In 1951 she became a member of the gymnastics section of the USSR Sports Committee; the following year she became head coach for the USSR women's gymnastics team and remained in that position until the mid-1960s. She was an internationally-ranked judge for many years, and became a member of the FIG's women's technical committee in 1966. She was a Merited Master of Sports and a Merited Coach of the USSR. She died in 2005.
