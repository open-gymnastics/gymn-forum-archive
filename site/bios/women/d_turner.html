Dumitrita Turner (ROM)

1979 World Championships Team and V Gold Medalist



Medal Total

World Championships: 2 Gold
Olympic Games:  1 Silver

Dumitrita (Dana) was born on February 12, 1964 in Onesti. She began gymnastics at age 7 and was coached by spouses Florica and Florin Dobre at the start of her career, then by the Karolyis. After retiring, she worked as a coach in Romania for a while and then coached the Guatemalan Olympic team. She met her Australian husband there, and they have been living in Australia for over 20 years. She's an accountant.
