Armine Barutyan (USSR)

1985 Medico Cup AA Champion

Armine was born in Yerevan, Armenian SSR, in 1971 or 1972. Her parents brought her to the School for Young Gymnasts (led by Olympic champion Grant Shaginyan) in 1977. Eventually, she transferred to Sports Boarding School No. 10 in Yerevan and trained at the Children's and Youth Sports School with coach Gaik Meltonyan. She earned the Master of Sports International Class title at the age of 11, thanks to her all-around win at the 1982 Druzhba competition. Unfortunately, her success in junior competitions did not transfer to success in senior competitions. Armine never finished in the top ten all-around at a USSR Championships or USSR Cup competition.

Citing her disillusionment with sports and the leadership of the Armenian SSR sports committee, Armine announced on the September 28, 1989, Sportzal TV program that she was moving permanently to the US. She began coaching at Verdugo Gymnastics in Los Angeles in 1990 and took a coaching position at Great American Gymnastics Express (GAGE) in Blue Springs, Missouri, several years later. She and GAGE's owner, Al Fong, eventually married and have a daughter, Athena, who was born in 2010.
