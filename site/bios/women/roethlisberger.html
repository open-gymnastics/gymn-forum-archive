Marie Roethlisberger (USA)

1982 Coca-Cola International AA Champion
1990 NCAA Uneven Bars Champion

Marie was born into a gymnastics family on May 12, 1966.  Her father, Fred, was a 1968 Olympian, while her brother John competed at the 1992 and 1996 Olympics.  Marie's best event was uneven bars; she was the alternate at the 1984 Olympics.  She competed for the University of Minnesota and graduated in 1991.  Marie attended medical school at Minnesota and received her degree in 1996.  She's a physician in Madison, Wisconsin.
