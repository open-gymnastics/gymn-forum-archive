Laura Muñoz (ESP)

1983 and 1987 Mediterranean Games AA Champion

Laura was born on June 9, 1970 in Madrid. She trained at the Calvo Munar Rodeira Club with coach Jesus Carballo. She's married to Angel Calzada and in 2005 was the Ambassador of the XV Mediterranean Games.
