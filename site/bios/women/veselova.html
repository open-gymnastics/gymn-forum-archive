Elena Veselova (USSR)

1982 Coca-Cola International AA Champion

Elena was born in 1965.  She was coached by Vladislav Rastorotsky at Dinamo in Rostov-on-Don.

Competition Results

1978
Jr. URS-SUI-ROM Match: 1st Team, 2nd AA

1980
RSFSR Cup: 6th AA

1981
RSFSR Championships: 3rd AA

1982
Coca-Cola International:  1st AA, 2nd UB, 2nd FX, 3rd V
RSFSR Championships:  1st AA, 1st V, 1st B, 1st FX
Southern Russia Region Qualification Competition: 1st AA
USSR Championships:  1st Team, 6th AA
USSR Cup:  5th AA

1983
University Games:  1st Team, 12th AA, 2nd V, 3rd FX
