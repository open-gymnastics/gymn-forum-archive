Li Ning (CHN)

1982 and 1986 World Cup AA Champion
1982 and 1986 Asian Games AA Champion

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  3 Gold, 1 Silver, 1 Bronze
World Championships:  2 Gold, 5 Silver, 4 Bronze
World Cup:  9 Gold, 2 Bronze

Li was born on October 3, 1963, in Liuzhou.  He began gymnastics and was coached by Zhang Jian.  A winner of 3 gold medals at the 1984 Olympic Games -- and two-time World Cup AA champion -- Li was expected to win more Olympic medals at the 1988 Games. Unfortunately, he injured his ankle during the team competition.  Li is now a successful businessman in China.  He is married to Chen Yongyan, herself a former Olympic gymnast.
