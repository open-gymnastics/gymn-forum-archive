Vitaly Scherbo (USSR/BLR)

1992 Olympic AA, PH, R, PB and V Champion, 1993 World AA Champion

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  6 Gold, 4 Bronze
World Championships:  12 Gold, 7 Silver, 4 Bronze
World Cup:  2 Gold, 1 Silver, 1 Bronze
European Championships:  9 Gold, 5 Silver, 2 Bronze

Vitaly was born on January 13, 1972, in Minsk.  He was coached by Sergei Shinkar and Leonid Filipenok.  Scherbo won 6 individual gold medals at the 1992 Olympic Games -- a record.  He now lives in Las Vegas with his wife, Irina, and daughter, Kristina.  He owns and runs the Vitaly Scherbo School of Gymnastics.
