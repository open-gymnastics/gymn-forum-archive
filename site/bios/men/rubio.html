Miguel Rubio (ESP)

1988 NCAA All-Around Champion
5-Time World Championships Competitor

Miguel was born on August 20, 1966. He began gymnastics at age 9 with coach Francisco Molina at Gimnasio Municipal de Cornella. Four years later, he transferred to Fuxarda Club, where he trained with Jose Alberola. He later attended Houston Baptist University and won the NCAA title in 1988. In March 1990, the team was given 3 years' probation by the NCAA for major violations.
