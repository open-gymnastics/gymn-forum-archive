Sorin Cepoi (ROM)

 

Cepoi was born on September 13, 1956, in Bucharest.  He married fellow gymnast Teodora Ungureanu in 1980.  The couple moved to France in the 1980s, and their gymnast Chloe Maigre was a member of the 1992 Olympic Team.  The couple eventually moved to the USA.  They started Dynamic Gymnastics in Mohegan Lake, NY, in 1995.  They coached national team member Sabrina Vega.
