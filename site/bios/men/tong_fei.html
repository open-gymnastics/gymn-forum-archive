Tong Fei (CHN)

1983 and 1985 World FX Champion

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  2 Silver
World Championships:  4 Gold, 1 Silver, 2 Bronze
World Cup:  1 Gold, 1 Silver, 3 Bronze

Fei was born on March 25, 1961, in Jiangxi Province and was discovered in 1972 by coach Zhang Jian.
