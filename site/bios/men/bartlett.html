Terry Bartlett (GBR)

1985 NCAA V Silver Medalist
3-Time Olympian
5-Time World Championships Competitor

Terry was born on December 2, 1963 in England.  After graduating from The Mountbatten School and Language College, he entered Penn State University on a full gymnastics scholarship
