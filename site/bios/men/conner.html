Bart Conner (USA)

1984 Olympic PB Champion
1979 World PB Champion
1979 World Cup PH Champion




Medal Total

Olympic Games:   2 Gold
World Championships:  1 Gold, 2 Bronze
World Cup:  1 Gold, 1 Silver

Conner was born on March 28, 1958.  As a teenager, he was already one of the USA's best gymnasts.  He won the 1976 American Cup and competed in the 1976 Olympics while still in high school.  He was a standout NCAA performer.  He won several medals internationally (World Championships and World Cup), but perhaps his greatest achievement was the gold medal he won for his PB routine at the 1984 Olympics.  Conner is married to Nadia Comaneci.  Their first child, son Dylan, was born in June 2006. They live in Norman, OK, where they own Bart Conner Gymnastics Academy and are editors of International Gymnast magazine.
