Sergei Khizhnyakov (USSR)

1977 Champions All AA Gold Medalist
1980 Moscow News AA Gold Medalist

Sergei was born in Donetsk in 1958. He began gymnastics in elementary school where his talent was spotted. He began serious training at a children's and youth sports school in his hometown and soon was invited to attend a new sports boarding school in Rostov. He eventually was named to the USSR's national junior team and later the senior team. He trained at the Army Club in Rostov-on-Don until his retirement from competitive gymnastics. He worked as a coach in Rostov and in Boston for a little while.
