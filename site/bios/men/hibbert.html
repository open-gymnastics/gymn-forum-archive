Curtis Hibbert (CAN)

1987 World HB Silver Medalist
1992 World V Bronze Medalist

Medal Total

World Championships:  1 Silver, 1 Bronze

Curtis was born on September 2, 1966, in Jamaica.  In 1972, he and his family emigrated to Canada.  He began gymnastics at age 9.  Tom Zivic was Curtis' coach from the age of 12.
