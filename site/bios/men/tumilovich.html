Alexander Tumilovich (USSR)

1981 Champions All AA Gold Medalist

Medal Total

World Championships:  1 Gold

Alexander was born on September 1, 1962 in Vitebsk (Belarussian SSR). He's been a team coach at Exxcel Gymnastics in Newton, MA, since its inception in 1998.
