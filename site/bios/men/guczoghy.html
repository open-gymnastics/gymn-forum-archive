György Guczoghy (HUN)

1979 and 1981 European PH Champion
1987 European AA Bronze Medalist

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  1 Bronze
World Championships:  1 Silver, 1 Bronze
European Championships:  3 Gold, 1 Silver, 6 Bronze

Guczoghy was born on March 3, 1962 in Budapest.  He was coached by Antal Kisteleki at Budapest Honved.  Gyorgy is married to former gymnast Lenke Almasi (who competed at the 1979 World Championships).
