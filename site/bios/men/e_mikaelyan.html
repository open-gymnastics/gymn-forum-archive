Eduard Mikaelyan (USSR)

1972 Olympic Team Silver Medalist

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  1 Silver
World Championships:  1 Silver

Eduard was born on May 25, 1950 in Yerevan and trained at Burevestnik.  Brother Ruben (born April 31, 1952) was also a member of the USSR senior national gymnastics team.
