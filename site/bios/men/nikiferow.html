Jan-Peter Nikiferow (GDR/GER)

1991 World Champion Team Bronze Medalist

Jan-Peter was born on November 8, 1971, in Magdeburg.  He trained at SC Dynamo Berlin.  Married to former gymnast Silvia Rau, they have a daughter named Jessika who was born in 1993.
