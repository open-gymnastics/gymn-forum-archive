Eugen Mack (SUI)

1928 Olympic V Champion
1934 World AA Champion

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  2 Gold, 4 Silver, 2 Bronze
World Championships:  5 Gold, 4 Silver, 1 Bronze

Eugen was born on September 21, 1907, in Arbon, Switzerland.  He died on October 29, 1978.

Competition Results

1928

Olympic Games:  1st Team, 1st V, 3rd HB

1934

World Championships:  1st AA, 1st PH, 1st V, 1st PB, 2nd FX, 2nd R

1936

Olympic Games:  2nd Team, 2nd AA, 2nd PH, 2nd V, 3rd FX

1938

World Championships:  2nd Team, 3rd AA, 1st V, 2nd FX
