Wilhelm Kubica (POL)

1969 European PH Champion

Medal Total

European Championships:  1 Gold, 1 Silver

Wilhelm was born on December 29, 1943 in Radlin, Poland.  His two younger brothers, Mikolai and Szylweszter, were also world-class gymnasts.  They were coached by Henrik Gaca in Krakow.  The three brothers competed together at the 1970 World Championships and the 1972 Olympics!
