Miguel Arroyo (CUB)

1983 Champions All AA Gold Medalist

 

Miguel was born on June 17, 1962 and was the youngest Cuban athlete at the 1980 Olympic Games.  His son, Irving, is also a world-class gymnast who finished 18th AA at the 2007 Pan American Games.
