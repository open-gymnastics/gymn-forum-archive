Vladimir Achasov (USSR)

1977 Coca-Cola Intl. AA Silver Medalist

Achasov was born in 1958 and was coached by P. Abutsaev at the Army Club. He married Olympic champion Nelli Kim in 1977; they were divorced in 1979.

Jr. USSR-ROM Dual Meet:  1st Team, 1st AA

1976

1977

USSR Championships:  2nd Team, 16th AA
