Shigeru Kasamatsu (JPN)

1974 World AA Champion
1978 World HB Champion
1979 World Cup AA Silver Medalist

Medal Total

Olympic Games:   1 Gold, 1 Silver, 2 Bronze
World Championships:  5 Gold, 2 Silver
World Cup:  1 Gold, 2 Silver

Kasamatsu was born on July 16, 1947, in Mie.  At age 10, he began taking a recreational tumbling class in the evenings, and by junior high school he was training in all of the gymnastics events.  He was the 1974 AA world champion but an emergency appendectomy kept him off the 1976 Olympic team.  His wife, Kazue Hanyu, is also a former Olympic gymnast.  Their son, Akihiro, is a member of Japan's national team.
