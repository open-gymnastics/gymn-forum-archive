Sergei Kharkov (URS/RUS/GER)

1988 Olympic V Champion
1993 World HB Champion

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  3 Gold
World Championships:  1 Gold, 1 Silver
European Championships:  2 Silver, 2 Bronze

Sergei was born on November 17, 1970, in Moscow.  He emigrated to Germany in 1994 and received his citizenship in 1997.  He and his wife, Natalia, live in Dilligen and have two boys (Sasha and Nikita).
