Adzhat Ibadulaev (URS)

1939 and 1940 USSR All-Around Champion
Five-time USSR Rings Champion

Adzhat was born in Ashgabat, Turkmenistan, in 1913. He first took up gymnastics while studying at the Automotive Technical College and began coaching the sport a year later. He moved to Moscow in 1935 to attend the Stalin Institute of Physical Culture in order to become a better coach. He continued his gymnastics training and learned a lot from watching other gymnasts work out. After only a year of training in Moscow with some of the top gymnasts, Ibadulaev won the city's championships. He worked towards his goal of one day participating at all-union competitions against his country's best gymnasts. Not only did he achieve that goal, he became one of the USSR's top gymnasts of the late 1930s to the early 1950s. He was one of the first Soviet athletes to earn the Master of Sports title. He died in 1995.
