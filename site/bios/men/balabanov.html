Yuri Balabanov (USSR)

1983 Coca Cola International AA Champion
1985 World R Bronze Medalist

Medal Total

World Championships:  1 Gold, 1 Bronze

Balabanov was born on February 2, 1963 in Vitebsk (Belorussian SSR) and trained at the Dynamo club in  Minsk.  He died on May 10, 2006.
