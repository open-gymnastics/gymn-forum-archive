Li Xiaoshuang (CHN)

1995 World AA Champion
1996 Olympic AA Champion

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  2 Gold, 2 Silver, 1 Bronze
World Championships:  3 Gold, 3 Silver

Li was born on April 9, 1973.  His twin brother, Dashuang, is also a gymnast and was a member of the silver-medal 1992 Olympic team.  Xiaoshuang means "big brother" and Dashuang means "little brother".
