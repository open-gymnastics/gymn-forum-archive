Igor Korobchinsky (USSR/UKR)

1989 and 1992 European AA Champion

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  1 Gold, 2 Bronze
World Championships:  6 Gold, 3 Silver, 1 Bronze
European Championships:  4 Gold, 4 Silver, 1 Bronze

Igor was born on August 16, 1969.  He trained at Dynamo Lugansk.
