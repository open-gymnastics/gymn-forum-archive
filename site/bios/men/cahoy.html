Phil Cahoy (USA)

1980 US Olympic Team Member
10-Time NCAA All-American

Gymnastics is a family affair in the Cahoy family.  Phil's father owned his own gym (Cahoy's School of Gymnastics) and was his son's coach.  Phil had a very successful high school career, leading his team to three state titles, and an equally successful college career.  He won several US apparatus titles and was a member of three World Championships teams, as well as a member of the 1980 Olympic team.  After a long gymnastics career, Cahoy attended medical school and became an orthopedic surgeon.  He lives with his family in Grand Island, Nebraska.
