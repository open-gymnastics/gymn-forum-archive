Alexander Tkachev (USSR)

1977 World Cup AA Bronze Medalist
1979 World AA Bronze Medalist
1980 Olympic PB Champion
1981 European AA Champion
1981 World HB Champion


Medal Total

Olympic Games:  2 Gold, 1 Silver
World Championships:  3 Gold, 3 Silver, 3 Bronze
World Cup:  1 Gold, 2 Silver, 3 Bronze
European Championships:  4 Gold, 5 Silver, 2 Bronze

Alexander was born on October 4, 1957 and trained at Dynamo Moscow.  He's married to Lidia Gorbik, and they coach at Dynamo Sanford in Sanford, FL.
