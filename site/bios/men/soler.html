Miguel Soler (ESP)

1984 Blume Memorial AA Champion

Miguel was born on June 6, 1960 in Barcelona, and began gymnastics at age 12. His two younger sisters participated in swimming. After retiring, he and two other gymnasts started performing acrobatics professionally in 1987. He lives in Las Vegas and has performed a hand-balancing act with Fulvia Sanchez (a rhythmic gymnast who represented Venezuela at the 1988 Pan American Games) .
