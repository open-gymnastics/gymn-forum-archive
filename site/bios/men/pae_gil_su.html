Pae Gil Su (PRK)

1992 Olympic PH Champion
1992, 1993 and 1996 World PH Champion

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  1 Gold
World Championships:  3 Gold, 1 Bronze

Pae was born on March 4, 1972.  He studied at the Korean Physical Education College.
