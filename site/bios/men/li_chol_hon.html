Li Chol Hon (PRK)

1986 Cottbus Intl. AA Champion
1986 Riga Intl. AA Champion

Li was born on October 4, 1962 in Sariwon.  He was inspired to start gymnastics at age 12 after seeing the 1974 World Championships on television.  His coach was Kim Zun Gil.  Li trained at the Locomotive Sports Club.
