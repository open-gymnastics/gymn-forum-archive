Valery Kerdemelidi (USSR)

1963 European AA Bronze Medalist

Medal Total

Olympic Games:   1 Silver
World Championships:  2 Silver
European Championships:  2 Silver, 3 Bronze

Valery was born on July 18, 1938.  A Merited Master of Sport, he trained at the Central Army Club in Moscow.  After retiring in 1968, Kerdemelidi worked as a coach at his club from 1972 to 1992.  He worked as a gymnastics commentator for Russian TV during the 1996 Atlanta Olympics.
