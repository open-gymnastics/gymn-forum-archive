Nobuyuki Kajitani (JPN)

1984 Olympic PB Silver Medalist

Medal Total

Olympic Games:   1 Silver, 1 Bronze
World Championships:  1 Silver, 1 Bronze
World Cup:  2 Bronze

Nobuyuki was born on May 3, 1955, in Saito City, Miyazaki.  He's a graduate of the Nippon College of Physical Education.
