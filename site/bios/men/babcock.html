Brian Babcock (USA)

1983 Pan American Games AA Silver Medalist
1985 USA AA Champion

Brian was born on July 11, 1960 in Garden City, Kansas.  Babcock graduated from Southern Illinois University in 1983 with a degree in education, and received a master's degree in education in 1989.  He was the boys' program director at the Parkettes Club from 1989 to 1996.  He moved to Texas in 1996 and started the Texas Sports Ranch with Kevin Mazeika, a former college teammate.  Babcock died of myelodysplasia on October 18, 1998.
