Grigory Misutin (USSR/UKR)

1991 AA World Champion, 1992 Olympic AA Silver Medalist

Medal Total

Olympics:  1 Gold, 4 Silver, 1 Bronze
World Championships:  4 Gold, 2 Bronze
European Championships:  1 Bronze

Grigory was born on December 29, 1970, in Alexandria (Ukrainian SSR).  He began gymnastics lesson in his hometown and transferred to a sports boarding school, in the city of Lugansk, when he was in the 4th grade.  In late 2004, he accepted a coaching job in Germany.
