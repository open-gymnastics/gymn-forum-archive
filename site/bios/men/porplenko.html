Yuri Porplenko (USSR)

1986 USSR Cup V Champion

Yuri was born in 1962 and trained at Dynamo in Simferopol.  He performed the triple somersault on floor exercise at the 1987 USSR Championships.

Competition Results

1981
USSR Championships:  2nd V

1984
All-Union Dynamo Championships:  3rd AA
USSR Championships:  8th AA

1985
USSR Cup:  2nd V

1986
USSR Cup:  1st V, 2nd FX
