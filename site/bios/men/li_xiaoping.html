Li Xiaoping (CHN)

1981 Pommel Horse World Champion

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  1 Gold
World Championships:  2 Gold, 1 Silver, 1 Bronze

At the 1984 Los Angeles Olympics, Li scored a perfect 10 on compulsory pommel horse.  Li owns South Coast Gymnastics in Irvine, CA, and is a coach there, along with his wife, Wen Jia.
