Alexei Voropaev (URS/RUS)

1992 and 1996 Olympic Team Gold Medalist
1992 Parallel Bars World Champion
1994 World Championships AA Silver Medalist

Medal Total

Olympic Games:  2 Gold
World Championships:  2 Gold, 2 Silver, 1 Bronze
European Championships:  1 Gold, 1 Bronze

Alexei was born on January 23, 1973 in Moscow.  He began gymnastics at age 6 and trained at the Dynamo Club.
