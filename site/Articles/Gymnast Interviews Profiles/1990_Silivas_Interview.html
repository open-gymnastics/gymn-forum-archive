Talking With Multiple Champion Daniela Silivas


Timpul. November 16, 1990. Taking advantage of the fact that, at the end of last wek, the multiple gymnastics champion Daniela Silivas was in Resita, I asked her to grant us an interview.

Q: How many times have you been to Resita and for what purpose?

A: I'm coming here for the third time. I was at the Romanian Nationals in 1985 and last year at the "Semenicul" Cup. This time I wanted to see my friends again, so let me tell you that I feel at home in Resita and that whenever the opportunity arises, I will do it.

Q: I know that you have an impressive number of medals obtained in world and Olympic competitions. I would like to ask you to list the most important ones.

A: I would not like you to think that I am modest, but there are many, I don't know if they are of interest and that we will have enough space in the magazine.

Q: We will find space for an athlete of such stature...

A: Okay. However, I will not go through the results obtained in the years 1982-1984 and I will start with my participation in a senior competition. It is about the 1985 Worlds, in Montreal, where I won the beam and came in second with the team, along with Kati Szabo, Laura Cutina, Camelia Voinea, Celestina Popa, and Eugenia Golea. At the World Cup, held in Beijing, I took second place in the all-around competition and the beam and third on the uneven bars. At the European Championships in Moscow in 1987 I won the all-around, uneven bars, beam and floor, and was second on the vault. The same year, the Rotterdam World Championships followed, where I won the uneven bars and floor exercise, third place in the individual all-around, and became champion with the team which was the same as the previous one (except minus Laura Cutina, who was replaced by Aurelia Dobre).

In 1988, I participated in the Seoul Olympics. There, I came second in the all-around and won beam, uneven bars, and floor exercise. Finally, last year's European Championships in Brussels brought me second place in the all-around, first place on the floor, second on the uneven bars, and third on the beam. At the World Championships in Stuttgart I won the beam, uneven bars, and floor exercise, while with the team I became world vice-champion.

Q: What a track record! Without a doubt, all these successes would not have been possible without a special talent and hard work and, last but not least, many great sacrifices. That's why, Daniela, I would like to ask you to tell me how and with whom you worked, how you reached your performance level?

A: I was in kindergarten. I lived in Deva. In fact, this is where I was born. I was only five years old when coaches Ulpia Barbu and Petre Serbu (my first coaches) came to make a selection. At first, there were a lot of us. I was among the few who remained. From the first grade, I had Ioan Carpinisan as my coach until the sixth grade, when I was included in the junior group that was being trained in Onesti. I returned to Deva, the center where the Olympic team was training, in 1984. I was training together with Ecaterina Szabo, Lavinia Agache, Cristina Grigoras, Aurelia Dobre, and Camelia Voinea, under the leadership of a group of coaches led by Adrian Goreac. This was until February of this year, when a curious decision, by someone else, led to the closure of the training center. Thus, all the girls returned to their clubs. After about a month, I went to France for knee surgery. I returned home and, although the surgery was successful, I gave up on competition.

Q: Suddenly, just like that? What was the reason?

A: After a fairly long period of interruption, the team reunited in Onesti, with other coaches, and I didn't want to go there anymore. That's on the one hand. On the other hand, you know, I had gotten used to Goreac. The same thing happened with Aurelia Dobre and Gabriela Potorac.

Q: You gave us sad news. Of course, you know best what to do. Tell us something about what you are doing now and in the future.

A: I am currently a student at the Faculty of Physical Education and Sports at the Ecological University of Bucharest. The expenses are borne by the federation and the ministry: 40,000 lei per year, so 200,000 lei for the five years of training. As for the future, I want to return to Deva, to become a coach. What more can I tell you?

Q: What are your interests in your free time, because now, giving up performance, you don't have to worry about that problem?

A: I'm a young woman like any other and it's natural that I like to read (but only crime books!), listen to music, dance...

Q: Daniela, you told me at the beginning that you don't like giving interviews and yet you told me interesting things...

A: I made an exception, and now I confess that I also enjoyed it.

Q: In that case, when will we see you again to continue the dialogue?

A: Maybe on New Year's Eve. I would like to do it in Resita.

Q: You are welcome!

MARIUS MORARU
