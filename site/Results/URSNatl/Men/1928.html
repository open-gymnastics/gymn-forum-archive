1928 USSR Championships

August 11-13
Moscow

In the first USSR Gymnastics Championships, gymnasts competed in these events: high bar, parallel bars, floor exercise, rope climb, long horse vault, long jump, 400m race and 100m race.  Sixty male gymnasts took part.
