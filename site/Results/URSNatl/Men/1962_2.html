1962 USSR Championships

November 21-28
Moscow

The  team competition was divided into 2 groups.  Group 1: Burevestnik (4 teams), Armed Forces (4 teams), Dinamo (4 teams), Spartak (4 teams), Trud (4 teams), Lokomotiv and Avangard (1 team each).  Group 2:  Sports Societies from the various republics.  Gymnasts competed optional routines only, and the top 14 after preliminaries advanced to the final round.
