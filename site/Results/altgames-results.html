Alternate Games

An "alternate" gymnastics competition was held on the two occasions of a boycotted Olympic Games. 

1980 Hartford, USA: Men's AA, Men's EF, Women's AA, Women's EF
1984 Olomouc, CSSR: Men's Team, Men's AA, Men's EF, Women's Team, Women's AA, Women's EF
