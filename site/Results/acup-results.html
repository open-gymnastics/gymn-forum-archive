American Cup

The American Cup was created to celebrate the 200th birthday of the USA.  It has since become one of the longest-running of the international invitational meets.  

In 1979, a mixed pairs competition was introduced (International Mixed Pairs).  

From 1996-1999, rhythmic gymnastics was added and this secondary meet was renamed the International 3-on-3.  

In 2000, gymnasts qualified to the American Cup from their results at two qualifying meets.  

In 2001, the event became a one-day competition.  

Another format change in 2005:  the meet became a World Cup qualifying meet, so only individual events were contested.  

In 2006, the format returned being an all-around competition with two days of competition (preliminaries and finals).  There were no awards or prize money given to event winners.

In 2008, the event became a one-day competition for the second time.
