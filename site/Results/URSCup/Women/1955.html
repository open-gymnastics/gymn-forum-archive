1955 USSR Cup

March 21
Moscow

Women's AA

Rank       AA Total
1 Sofia Muratova Moscow 38.20
2 Ludmila Egorova Leningrad 38.20
3 Tamara Manina Leningrad 38.00
4 Larissa Dirii Kiev 37.85
5 Maria Gorokhovskaya Kherson 37.80
6 Z.  Ruleva Moscow 36.20

The first Cup competition was organized by the Committee for Physical Culture and Sport.  Only the compulsory routines were performed, and they were judged according to international rules.  Seven men and eight women were invited to compete.

Muratova was declared the winner because she had the highest scores on 3 of the 4 events.
