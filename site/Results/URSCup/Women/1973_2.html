1973 USSR Cup

September 17-23
Tbilisi

The USSR Cup was held twice in 1973.  In the first competition (in April), 48 of the country's best male and female gymnasts competed for AA and EF titles.  In the second competition, in September, 24 male and female gymnasts vied for AA and EF titles.
