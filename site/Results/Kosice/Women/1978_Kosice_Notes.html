1978 Kosice International

Competition Notes

  • Larissa Milovanova (USSR) performed a double back tuck on FX as her first and last passes

  • Tatiana Arzhannikova (USSR) performed a full-twisting Tsukahara and piked front in V finals

  • Arzhannikova performed a free hip full pirouette and full-twisting Comaneci dismount in UB finals

  • Milivanova performed a front somi mount; round-off to layout step-out, and double tuck dismount in B finals

  • Leslie Pyfer performed a piked Tsukahara and tucked front in V finals

  • Leslie Pyfer performed a gainer back tuck; front somi; and FF, FF, double twist dismount in B finals
