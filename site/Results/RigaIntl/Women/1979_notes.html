1979 Riga International

Notes

Women's Competition

  • Kiss (ROM) tumbled a double pike, double back and a double twist on FX.

  • Glushchenko (URS) dismounted UB with a hecht back somersault.

  • Kiss (ROM) performed a jump to immediate back tuck on B.

  • In B finals, Filatova (URS) performed a FF, LO, FF.

  • In FX finals, Filatova (URS) opened with a full-in and ended with a double pike.

  • Kiss (ROM) and Glushchenko (URS) both fell while attempting a double pike during FX finals.
