US Olympic Festival

This competition, originally called the National Sports Festival, ceased to exist after 1995.

1978:  Men's AA/EF, Women's AA/EF
1979:  Men's AA/EF, Women's AA/EF
1981:  Women's AA/EF (UPDATED!), Men's AA/EF  NEW!
1982:  Men's AA/EF, Women's AA/EF
1986:  Men's AA/EF, Women's AA/EF
1987:  Men's AA, Women's AA
1989:  Men's AA, Women's AA
1990:  Men's AA, Women's AA
1991:  Men's AA, Women's AA
1993:  Men's AA, Women's AA
1994:  Men's AA, Women's AA
1995:  Men's AA, Women's AA

The 1989 women's competition doubled as the U.S. junior national championships.
