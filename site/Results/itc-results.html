International Team Championships

This was an annual 3-nation team meet between China, Romania and the USA.  In 1999, Australia competed in the senior women's division.  In 2001 the name of this competition was changed to the American Team Cup.

1997:  Sr. Men, Sr. Women, Jr. Men, Jr. Women
1998:  Sr. Men, Sr. Women, Jr. Men, Jr. Women
1999:  Sr. Men, Sr. Women, Jr. Men, Jr. Women
2000:  Men, Women
2001:  Men, Women
