1978 Moscow News

Notes

Women's Competition

  • Schwandt (USA) received a 0.1 deduction for being under time on B during AA.

  • Kardos (USA) fell off B after a back walkover, back tuck.

  • Schwandt vaulted a layout Tsukahara and a tuck front.

  • Filatova (URS) and Mukhina (URS) vaulted a layout Tsukahara and a full-twisting tucked Tsukahara in EF.

  • Mukhina tumbled a full-in and a 1.5 twist through to double back on FX.

  • Schwandt attempted a double tuck on FX during EF.

  • An unnamed gymnast on the USSR's alternate team tumbled a front handspring, Rudi, FF, FF, double back.

  • An unnamed gymnast on the USSR's alternate team performed a front handspring, front tuck on B.
