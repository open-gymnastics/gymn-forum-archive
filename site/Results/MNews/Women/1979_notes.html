1979 Moscow News

Notes

Women's Competition

  • Anderson (USA) performed a front tuck on V; Zakharova (URS) performed a pike front 1/2 on V.

  • Anderson received the "most original element" award for her layout full twist, punch front on FX.

  • Wirth (USA) performed a full-twisting tucked Tsukahara in V finals.

Men's Competition

  • Felix Roche (CUB) performed a handspring double front on V.

  • Casimiro Suarez (CUB) attempted a double-twisting double back dismount on HB.
