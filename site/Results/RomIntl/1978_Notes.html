1978 International Championships of Romania

Competition Notes

  • Bogdan Makuts (USSR) performed a flyaway 1/2 twist straddle pike (Deltchev?) and a tucked full-in full-out dismount on HB

  • Andrew Lev (USSR) performed a Tkachev on HB

  • Makuts opened on FX with a tucked full-in and ended with a double back

  • Kurt Thomas (USA) performed piked Tsukahara and a tucked full-twisting Tsukahara in V finals
