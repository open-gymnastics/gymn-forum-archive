1982 Coca-Cola International

Notes

Women's Competition

  • Hayley Price (GBR) vaulted a full-on, full-off.  In event finals, she performed a full-on, 1 1/2 off.

  • Luci Collins (USA) vaulted a Cuervo.

  • Elena Veselova (URS) vaulted a layout Yurchenko.

  • On bars, Marie Roethlisberger (USA) performed a Tkachev to straddle back to handstand on low bar.

  • On beam, Liu Ruixia (CHN) performed a dismount of aerial cartwheel, layout stepout, full twist.

  • Roethlisberger's floor music was "Rhapsody in Blue."

Men's Competition

  • Stepan Martsinkiv (URS) performed a layout full-in to piked back out on floor.

  • Taichi Okada (JPN), Xiong Songliang (CHN) and Lui Ming (CHN) performed a double layout on floor.

  • On vault, Casimiro Suarez (CUB) performed a handspring front 1/2 to score 9.95 in event finals.

  • On high bar, Suarez performed a one-arm giant to Deltchev and attempted a full-twisting Gienger.

  • Martsinkiv performed a one-arm front giant to Jaeger on high bar and dismounted with a double full-in, back out.
