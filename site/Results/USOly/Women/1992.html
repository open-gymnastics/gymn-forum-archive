The top seven finishers at the Trials, plus Betty Okino, took part in a training camp in Orlando, FL, on July 7-9.  There, Kim Kelly was cut from the US Olympic Team and Okino (who had competed in neither the US Championships nor Olympic Trials due to injury) was named to the team instead.
