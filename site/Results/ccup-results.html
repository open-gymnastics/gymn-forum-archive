Chunichi Cup

The Chunichi Cup celebrated its 30th anniversary in 2000!  It's the only annual international invitational meet to be held in Asia, and it's one of the most popular meets as well.  The Chunichi Cup has consistently attracted top gymnasts -- Nadia Comaneci, Lyudmila Turischeva, Nikolai Andrianov, Mary Lou Retton and Vitaly Scherbo are a few of the Olympic champions who have won this competition.
