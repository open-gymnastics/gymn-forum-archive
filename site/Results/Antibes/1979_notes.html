1979 Antibes International

Notes

Anca Kiss (ROM)

  • Vaulted a tucked full-twisting Tsukahara, with a low landing.

  • Beam elements included leap to immediate FF, back tuck; back walkover, back tuck; RO double back dismount.

  • Floor music was "Oh My Darling Clementine".  Tumbles were double pike; double twist; double tuck.

Katarina Rensch (GDR)

  • Vaulted a handspring, front tuck and a piked Tsukahara.

  • Performed a side salto on beam (forward take-off, sideward landing).

  • Used orchestrated music on floor; she opened with a double pike and ended with a double twist.

Zhu Zheng (CHN)

  • Vaulted a Tsukahara.

  • Beam dismount was a round off, layout, immediate back salto.

  • Floor tumbles were double back (fall); Arabian, RO, FF, layout; double twist.

Ma Wenchu (CHN)

  • Opened on Floor with a tucked full-in (fall).

Katalin Ujzsigeti (HUN)

  • Vaulted a handspring full and a piked Tsukahara.

  • Beam elements included 2 back tucks; front aerial, side aerial; cartwheel, FF; 3 FF in a row; Tsukahara pike dismount.

  • On floor, she opened with a double back and ended with a double twist.

Leslie Russo (USA)

  • Vaulted a layout Tsukahara.

  • On beam, Russo fell after a FF, layout and dismounted with a double pike.

  • Tumbled a double back; full twist; double twist.

Suzy Kellems (USA)

  • Vaulted a layout Tsukahara.

  • On floor, she tumbled a double back; Arabian, front handspring, front salto; full twist.
