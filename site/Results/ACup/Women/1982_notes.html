1982 American Cup

 Competition Notes

  • Tatiana Frolova (URS) vaulted a full-twisting layout Tsukahara.

  • Albina Shishova (URS) performed a round-off, full-twisting tucked back on B.

  • Tracee Talavera (USA) mounted B with a round-off, flip-flop, flip-flop, layout.

  • Tunde Zsilinski (HUN) dismounted B with a back walkover, 2 flip-flops, Tsukahara.

  • On FX, Zoya Grancharova (BUL) tumbled a triple twist, front through to double tuck and another double tuck to close.

  • Albina Shishova (URS) attempted a full-in on floor.

  • Alexander Pogorelov (URS) performed a 1 1/2 front somi on PB.
